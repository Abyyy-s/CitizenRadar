Calibration steps

1. Open the Live Monitor.

2. Click Calibrate road points.

3. Select the four corners of the road on the video feed.

4. Enter the real road width and length.

5. Save the calibration and check the speed measurements.

Road dimensions

Reference information

Enter measurements in metres on the Live Monitor calibration panel. Use real-world dimensions for the selected road section. Incorrect dimensions can make speed estimates inaccurate.
